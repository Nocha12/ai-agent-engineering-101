# Goods counter and payment scheduling bundle

Instruction 68 replaces the proposed fresh-only next window with one bundled
treatment. This module is source-OFF. Its future flag, study and full thirty-minute
window are bound with its code to a literal content-addressed import by
`tools.fresh_release.files_from_git(bundle_settings=...)`. Existing fresh source
settings and their historical window remain unchanged.

A and B run the same complete immutable code/tool/interpreter/dependency release.
New normal goods orders using the resolved rule engine are assigned before stock
selection. Retries retain the deterministic arm; agent/resume/conflicting trials
keep baseline behavior. Assignment includes orders with no stock or no payment.

B combines two changes; their individual effects cannot be identified:

* Within fitted exploitation candidates, rank recent known payment outcomes for
  the same category and prospective SKU. One matching official payment joins one
  settlement by order, URL, SKU and amount. The memory's display name is not a
  settlement URL. Unknown outcomes are excluded from this historical ranking and
  reported, rather than treated as failed payments. At least three resolved
  observations are required. Payment probability has a common Beta(1,1) prior;
  mature saved quality has two prior observations at .25. The score is payment
  probability times (.5 + .5 times saved quality). Quality is observed only after
  the reported warranty, at least six hours; observed breaks contribute zero.
  This does not establish completed refunds. Stable ties, SKU/rival partitions,
  fresh exploration positions, canonical selection and later preference order
  are preserved. Actual first dispatch is recorded separately.
* Keep the first .90 offer. After it, offer at most .92 of the first ask once,
  bounded by the original value limit; accept only within that cap, otherwise
  walk away. Existing text parsing, warranty, budget and receipt guards stay in
  force. Unparsed text retains the existing harness path. The proposed .95 was
  an observed final/first-ask ratio, not a parameter present in baseline code.

Only the existing filesystem observer is extended with an explicit bundle event
root/protocol. Common eligibility means a pretreatment code-counter opportunity
or a different first fitted batch, rather than fresh-slot eligibility. Quote
thread start, history offer attempt, RPC, acknowledgement, paid and official
settlement are distinct. Missing/pending/refund/LLM attribution remain unknown.
The shared Players board is descriptive, not an arm-level causal estimate.

No operating result is implied by local replay. The suggested evaluation bars
remain +2 percentage points mature saved, no more than 10% lower settlement rate,
and 200 official settlements per arm with goods maturity. A short window below
those bars is inconclusive; it does not justify permanent adoption. Live source
seller/pricing/meta/dirty files must survive object-only delivery. The protected
runner uses only the existing official kit `join --dir` selector integration.
Independent final review, real consumer checks, safe retained rollback and the
normal operating gate precede any activation or restart.
